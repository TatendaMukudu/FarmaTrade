## P0.6 and RFC state

P0.6 remains blocked on RFC #21 Q1: whether the physical source is a hard
ceiling that prevents commitments across every linked intent, or whether
over-offering is allowed and surfaced. The two documented probes still state
the current defect: 40,000 kg can be promised against 26,000 kg of physical
stock, and replacing a withdrawn derived intent does not account for the live
agreement on the old intent.

No source-cap schema or allocation behavior is proposed here. RFC #21 Q2-Q6
also remain open: sourceless intents, fulfilment reconciliation, cancellation
authority, the overcommitment resolution path, and transition provenance.
RFC #20 Q2, Q5, Q6 and Q7 remain live separately. INV-10 remains violated,
INV-13 and INV-15 remain partial, and divergences D1-D6 remain open.

## Work boundary

The safe unblocked change found by this audit was the verification defect:
the database-free typecheck depended on generated files left by `next dev` or
`next build`, even though a fresh checkout has neither. Next 16 documents
`next typegen` as the command that generates these global route helpers
without a full build, so the gate can create its own prerequisite rather than
depend on workstation history.

P0.6 allocation behavior, role-separated reputation, cancellation semantics,
review timing, Home/Trade/Network restructuring, demand-side initiation, and
contact/messaging unlock rules all require product or RFC decisions (or a
separately scoped phase). They must not be inferred from this audit.
